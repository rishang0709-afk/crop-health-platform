# Crop Health Early Warning & Management System

## Problem

Farmers often detect crop diseases and pest infestations only after
visible damage has already spread.

Our system aims to provide timely, reliable and locally relevant
crop-health detection, risk forecasting and management support.

## Target Users

1. Farmers
2. Extension Workers
3. Agriculture Officials
4. Agricultural Experts

## Core Goal

Detect crop diseases and pest infestations early and provide
actionable, locally relevant management recommendations.

## MVP Features

### Farmer

- Registration and login
- Farmer profile
- Farm/field registration
- Crop registration
- Crop image upload
- Basic symptom/context input
- Disease/pest identification
- Confidence score
- Severity estimation
- Weather-based risk score
- Management recommendations
- Detection history
- GPS-based reports

### Extension Worker

- Login
- Regional dashboard
- Disease reports
- Disease hotspot map
- High-risk cases
- Expert review workflow

### Expert

- Review AI predictions
- Confirm diagnosis
- Correct diagnosis
- Add expert comments
- Provide recommendations
